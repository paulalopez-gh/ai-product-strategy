# Cost Curve & Pricing Strategy

|--------------|----------------|-------|

| Human-in-the-loop | $0.15| Manager review for low-confidence and exception cases|
| **Total AI COGS** |$0.24 | Estimated blended cost per customer/month|

## Cascading Strategy
<!-- Cheap model → frontier model routing logic -->

**Triage model:** GPT-4o Mini (Azure OpenAI) or equivalent low-cost model
**Frontier model:** GPT-4o (Azure OpenAI) with Document Intelligence for complex emails and attachments
**Routing rule:** Low-cost model handles classification, extraction, and confidence scoring. High-risk, low-confidence, complex attachment, or high-value orders are routed to the frontier model and, if necessary, to human review.
**Expected cascade ratio:** 75% triage model / 25% frontier model

## Pricing Model

**Current pricing:** Internal operational process. No direct customer pricing. Value is measured through cost reduction, faster order processing, and scalability of Customer Service operations.
**Proposed AI pricing:** Internal chargeback based on automated orders processed and operational savings generated.
**Model:** outcome-based

## Stress Tests

| Scenario | Impact on Margin | Response |
|----------|-----------------|----------|
| Inference costs 3x |Low-Medium impact. Margins decrease but remain positive because AI costs are a small fraction of the operational savings generated by automating manual order processing. | Prioritize smaller, high-volume orders, optimize prompts, use model routing, and automate only high-confidence cases.|
| Heaviest segment doubles |Positive impact. The largest segment (reload orders, 70% of volume) benefits most from economies of scale because processing costs grow slower than operational savings. |Expand automation coverage for reload orders and continuously refine workflows using human feedback data. |
| Model provider raises prices 50% |Medium impact. Operating costs increase, but dependency on a single model vendor becomes the main risk. | Create a model-agnostic architecture using Azure AI Foundry and maintain the ability to switch between models/providers while preserving business logic and workflows.|

## Board One-Pager
<!-- Before/After: Old SaaS revenue vs. AI usage revenue for your product -->

**Before (traditional SaaS):** 100% of customer orders processed manually by Customer Service agents.
Cost scales linearly with order volume.
Average handling time driven by email review, validation, data entry in TRI2, and customer confirmation.
**After (AI-enabled):** 85% of low-complexity orders automated end-to-end.
AI classifies emails, extracts data from PDFs/Excel files, validates requests, and prepares or executes orders in TRI2.
Human review focused on exceptions, high-value orders, and complex accounts.
Cost per additional order approaches AI inference cost rather than employee cost.
**Net margin shift:** Significant positive margin expansion driven by operational cost reduction.
Target: ≥15% reduction in order processing time within the first 6 months.
Marginal cost growth becomes substantially lower than order volume growth.
Productivity gains increase without equivalent growth in Customer Service headcount.
